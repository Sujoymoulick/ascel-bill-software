# GST scope and offline issuance

Checked 25 November 2026 in Indian time. This is product research for domestic sale of goods.

## Which invoices can the first release issue offline?

### Takeaway

Start with regular GST-registered merchants with one GSTIN, one issuing device, and ordinary domestic sales of taxable goods outside mandatory e-invoicing. Support straightforward interstate and intrastate B2B and B2C after a chartered accountant validates the release fixtures. Ordinary invoices can be issued from local data when an IRN does not apply. A sale and shipment can have separate compliance requirements.

### Cited Findings

- CGST Act sections 31–33 govern issue of invoices and collection of tax. For ordinary taxable goods, invoice timing is before or at removal when movement is involved, or before or at delivery or making goods available otherwise. Exempt-only and composition supplies generally use a bill of supply under section 31(3)(c) and Rule 49. Section 31(3)(b) has a limited consolidation option for eligible small transactions where the unregistered recipient does not ask for an invoice. [India Code CGST Act](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_2_2_00042_201712_1517807328102&filename=a2017-12.pdf&type=actfile), [CBIC sectoral guidance](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/sectoral-faq.html)
- Rule 46 fields for ordinary taxable goods include supplier legal name, address, GSTIN, date, consecutive invoice number up to 16 characters, unique for the financial year, using letters, numerals, slash and hyphen, registered buyer name/address/GSTIN or UIN, HSN, item description, quantity and unit/UQC, gross and taxable values after applicable discount/abatement, GST rates and amounts by component, interstate place of supply and state information, different delivery address where relevant, reverse-charge indicator, and signature or digital signature as applicable. For an unregistered buyer, capture name, address, delivery address, state and code from ₹50,000 of taxable supply, and below that on request. Rule 46 contains an exception to signature for electronic invoices issued in accordance with the Information Technology Act. Rule 48 prescribes recipient, transporter and supplier copies for ordinary goods invoices, with a separate e-invoice regime. [CBIC consolidated Rules 46–49](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf), [CBIC interstate place-of-supply circular](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/circular-cgst-90.pdf)
- For invoice HSN, Notification 78/2020 applies 4 digits for taxpayers with preceding-year turnover up to ₹5 crore, with a B2C invoice exception for this group, and 6 digits above ₹5 crore. GST portal return tables have separate rules. Table 12 of GSTR-1/1A has distinct B2B and B2C HSN summaries, and document reporting in Table 13 is mandatory starting with May 2025 returns when supplies are reported. [CBIC HSN release](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/press-release/Press-release-HSN-code-SAC-issue.pdf), [GSTN Table 12 and 13 advisory](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/updated_advisory_hsn_table12_25042025.pdf)

### Inferences

- Require a merchant to confirm GST registration and applicability with their accountant. A locally validated GSTIN checksum or state prefix can catch input errors. It cannot verify that the registration is active. Keep verification status/date separate from entry validation.
- Drafts may change. Assign the final financial-year number atomically at issuance with its line snapshots, totals, payment or inventory event as applicable, and audit record. Make repeated issue commands idempotent. Never reuse a cancelled number. Ship a draft watermark and separate print variants where applicable. Exports for the accountant should be clearly named sales register and summary, with no implication that a portal return has been filed.
- Defer e-invoice-mandated customers, composition, exempt/mixed-document handling, cess or retail-sale-price-based valuation where unsupported, SEZ/export, reverse charge, complex third-party bill-to/ship-to, and multi-device writing. If a pilot merchant needs these to cover normal trading, qualify them out or revise scope explicitly.

### Gaps

- Reconfirm the legal treatment and delivery requirements of paper versus digitally issued PDFs, and the exact live return schema, before release. A browser mock is not a compliance test.

## How should GST calculation, corrections and records work?

### Takeaway

Determine tax heads from supplier location and legal place of supply, with traceable inputs and deterministic money calculation. Preserve issued snapshots and handle supported corrections through linked documents and an audit trail.

### Cited Findings

- IGST Act sections 7, 8 and 10 define interstate, intrastate and place of supply. For ordinary registered-buyer sales involving movement, place of supply generally follows where movement ends for delivery. Third-party directions under section 10(1)(b), no-movement transactions and on-site installation have distinct rules. For unregistered buyers, section 10(1)(ca) uses the invoice-recorded address or supplier location if unrecorded, with the state name sufficient for that subsection. CBIC clarifies that where billing and delivery addresses are both recorded and differ, use the delivery address in the cited unregistered-buyer case. For supported intrastate sales, show CGST plus SGST or UTGST where applicable, and IGST for interstate. [India Code IGST Act](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_2_2_00043_201713_1517807328116&filename=a2017-13.pdf&type=actfile), [CBIC Circular 209/03/2024](https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-09/circular-no-209-03-2024.pdf)
- CGST Act section 15 defines transaction value in the ordinary unrelated-party, sole-consideration case and treatment of eligible invoiced discounts and incidental charges. Section 170 rounds amounts payable/refundable under the Act to the nearest rupee with 50 paise rounding upward. The notified IRP schema has component totals plus a separate round-off field. This does not prescribe rounding every invoice line to whole rupees. [India Code CGST Act](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_2_2_00042_201712_1517807328102&filename=a2017-12.pdf&type=actfile), [IRP notified schema](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/notified-e-invoice-schema/)
- Section 34 permits credit notes for overcharged value/tax, returns and deficiency, and requires debit notes where taxable value/tax was understated. The section 34(2) credit-note declaration deadline is 30 November following the FY of supply or the date the relevant annual return is furnished, whichever is earlier. Reduction is subject to statutory conditions. Rule 53(1A) specifies note metadata, including corresponding original document references. [CBIC section 34](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-act/1000304/1000001), [CBIC Rule 53](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000144/1000001)
- Rule 56(8) calls for electronic edit and deletion logs for covered records, and Rule 56(15) addresses authentication of electronic records. Section 36 record retention is normally 72 months from the annual-return due date for the relevant year, with longer retention for specified proceedings/investigations. [CBIC Rule 56](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000149/1000001), [India Code CGST Act sections 35–36](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_2_2_00042_201712_1517807328102&filename=a2017-12.pdf&type=actfile)
- Rate changes are recent and ongoing. The 2025 reform moved many goods to main 5% and 18% slabs and introduced a special 40% rate on selected supplies. The public release discusses further 2026 changes, and the NIC/IRP explicitly lists a different calculation validation for certain retail-sale-price-based HSN from 1 February 2026. Do not use a static list of historic slabs or infer exact applicable rate solely from the most common category. [Ministry of Finance October 2026 briefing](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=160401&lang=2&reg=48), [NIC live announcements](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/announcements.html)

### Inferences

- Keep fixed-point decimal or integer paise plus sufficient intermediate precision for fractional quantities, inclusive pricing and components. Apply documented, accountant-approved rounding boundaries. Keep optional invoice-level cash round-off as a separately visible field and separately calculate statutory reporting amounts. Golden test example, taxable base ₹1,000 at 18% yields ₹90 CGST plus ₹90 SGST for a supported intrastate example, or ₹180 IGST on a supported interstate supply. ₹1,180 inclusive at the same simple rate solves to the same base. Test ₹0.49/₹0.50 at the applicable section 170 boundary.
- Use effective-dated rates, user confirmation of HSN/rate for the initial catalog, and immutable snapshot of rate/version, rounding rule and place of supply. Invoice output, sales register, inventory movement and credit/refund records should reconcile after restart and backup restore. For minor clerical corrections, cancellation or return amendments, route the user through an explicit accountant-signed policy. No universal direct edit or delete button for issued documents.

### Gaps

- The in-force status of all Finance Act 2026 post-sale discount or credit-note changes was outside the verified fixtures. Avoid automatic post-sale discounts and tax liability adjustments in the first scope until counsel signs off.

## How should e-invoice, e-way bill, and outages be handled?

### Takeaway

Treat mandatory IRN as a pre-issue gate and e-way bill as a separate dispatch gate. On connectivity failure, save work locally and continue transactions outside the applicable government gates. Use official alternate portals when available.

### Cited Findings

- Notification 13/2020 as amended by 10/2023 uses aggregate PAN-wide turnover **exceeding ₹5 crore in any preceding financial year from 2017–18** for the notified class, effective 1 August 2023, with specified exemptions. GSTN's summary sometimes uses shorthand '₹5 crore and above'; the operative Gazette retains 'exceeds'. GSTN says technical enablement alone does not establish whether reporting is legally required. Covered outbound transactions include B2B and exports, with applicable invoices, debit notes and credit notes. Ordinary B2C is outside this IRN flow. [Notification 10/2023 amending threshold](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/GST-notification-10-central-tax-english-2023.pdf), [Consolidated Notification 13/2020 via ICAI](https://d23z1tp9il9etb.cloudfront.net/download/gstlaw/NOTIFICATION%20NO.%2013_2020-CENTRAL%20TAX%20%5BG.S.R.%20196%28E%20-%20Copy1728968655.pdf), [GSTN e-invoicing guide](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/e_invoice_overview.pdf)
- Rule 48(4)–(5) requires a covered invoice to obtain an IRN and does not recognize a contrary issuance as an invoice. Official GSTN workflow sends the document to an authorized IRP and prints the signed QR received from that portal before issuing to the buyer. For AATO ₹10 crore and above, a portal restriction effective 1 April 2025 blocks IRN generation outside the 30-day window for invoices and covered notes. The restriction is separate from whether an IRN is mandatory at issue. IRP generally permits cancellation within 24 hours, subject to portal constraints. [CBIC Rule 48](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf), [GSTN e-invoicing guide](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/e_invoice_overview.pdf), [GSTN 30-day advisory copy](https://d23z1tp9il9etb.cloudfront.net/download/gstlaw/download1731066652.pdf), [Authorized IRP cancellation guidance](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/how-will-recipients-receive-e-invoices-from-their-suppliers/)
- Notification 04/2026–Central Tax, G.S.R. 918(E), adds specified inbound reverse-charge purchases from unregistered suppliers and imports of services for the notified taxpayer class from 1 January 2027. It does not change the threshold text or remove the proposed MVP’s need to exclude these cases. [Gazette document mirror](https://a2ztaxcorp.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/CBIC-NN-04-of-2026%E2%80%93-Central-Tax.pdf), [Ministry of Finance recommendations](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2320934&lang=1&reg=48)
- Rule 138 generally requires an e-way bill for goods consignment value exceeding ₹50,000, before commencement of movement, subject to exceptions, certain below-threshold cases, and applicable state/UT rules for intrastate movement. Consignment value generally includes applicable GST and cess, excluding exempt goods value when the same invoice covers exempt and taxable goods. Supplier, recipient or transporter may need to generate it depending on the transaction, and the person in charge carries the required invoice/challan and e-way evidence. For example, West Bengal reduced its intrastate threshold to over ₹50,000 from 1 June 2026, with an intrastate job-work exemption, superseding the older ₹1 lakh public material. [GSTN taxpayer welcome kit](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/welcome_kit_for_new_taxpyers.pdf), [NIC e-way bill FAQ](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/html/faq1.html), [J&K official explainer](https://enyayasarathi.jk.gov.in/fandq.php), [West Bengal Trade Circular 01/2026](https://comtax.wb.gov.in/GST/GST_Trade_Circulars/WBGST_TC_01_2026_20260525.pdf)
- GSTN and NIC document alternate IRP and e-way bill portals for continuity. An offline bulk spreadsheet can prepare data, but it cannot issue an authorized IRN offline. NIC’s current announcements also note that the contemplated mandatory ship-to GSTIN and closure changes were put on hold in July 2026. [GSTN continuity advisory via authorized IRP](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/gstn-issues-advisory-on-business-continuity-for-e-invoice-and-e-waybill-systems/), [NIC continuation portal](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/Documents/NIC_ewaybill2_Advisory.pdf), [NIC current announcements](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/announcements.html)

### Inferences

- Use separate document and dispatch statuses, e.g. draft, issued, corrected or cancelled, and e-way bill review, required/pending or reference recorded. For e-way bill MVP workflow, assist the merchant in using the official portal and recording the reference; explicitly separate merchant-entered and portal-verified statuses. Keep external IDs as strings and avoid hardcoded numeric widths, which can change. Support exemptions through documented, state-aware rules or a reviewed decision.
- In a later IRP connector, use a persistent request state with idempotency and reconciliation by GSTIN, invoice date/type and document number. A network timeout can mean the request succeeded. Look up before resubmitting or changing providers, retain signed payload plus acknowledgment and QR, and use an online rejection/ready status. No generic fail-open. GSTN has published 2025 case-insensitive IRN document numbering, so canonical uppercase deserves an explicit validation rule. [IRP lookup API](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/core-apis-wiki/), [Authorized IRP news](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/news/)

### Gaps

- Confirm the current state matrix for actual pilot locations, and go-live dates of any expected 2027 e-way bill identifier format changes with the official portal before implementing strict length checks. The scope here does not require a national eligibility engine.
